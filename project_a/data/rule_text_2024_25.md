# Income-tax provisions for salaried individuals, FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26)

## A. Common rules
1. Rounding (Sec 288A, 288B): total taxable income is rounded to the nearest multiple of Rs 10. The final tax payable is also rounded to the nearest multiple of Rs 10. Half-up rounding (5 rounds up).
2. Health and education cess (Finance Act 2024): 4% of (income-tax after rebate + surcharge).
3. Calculation order: gross salary, standard deduction, other deductions (old regime only), taxable income (rounded), slab tax, Sec 87A rebate, surcharge with marginal relief, cess, final tax (rounded).

## B. New regime (Sec 115BAC(1A))
4. Standard deduction (Sec 16(ia)): Rs 75,000, or the salary if lower.
5. No other deductions are allowed. HRA, 80C, 80CCD(1B), 80D and home-loan interest on a self-occupied house are NOT allowed.
6. Slabs on taxable income: up to Rs 3,00,000 nil; 3,00,001 to 7,00,000 at 5%; 7,00,001 to 10,00,000 at 10%; 10,00,001 to 12,00,000 at 15%; 12,00,001 to 15,00,000 at 20%; above 15,00,000 at 30%.
7. Rebate (Sec 87A): if taxable income is up to Rs 7,00,000, rebate equals the tax, up to Rs 25,000. Marginal relief: if taxable income exceeds Rs 7,00,000, the tax payable cannot exceed the income above Rs 7,00,000.
8. Surcharge: 10% if taxable income exceeds Rs 50 lakh, 15% above Rs 1 crore, 25% above Rs 2 crore. The rate is capped at 25% in this regime.

## C. Old regime
9. Standard deduction (Sec 16(ia)): Rs 50,000, or the salary if lower.
10. Slabs, age below 60: up to Rs 2,50,000 nil; 2,50,001 to 5,00,000 at 5%; 5,00,001 to 10,00,000 at 20%; above 10,00,000 at 30%.
11. Slabs, age 60 to 79: nil up to Rs 3,00,000; then 5% to 5,00,000; 20% to 10,00,000; 30% above.
12. Slabs, age 80 and above: nil up to Rs 5,00,000; 20% to 10,00,000; 30% above.
13. Rebate (Sec 87A): if taxable income is up to Rs 5,00,000, rebate equals the tax, up to Rs 12,500. There is no marginal relief for this rebate in the old regime.
14. Surcharge: 10% above Rs 50 lakh, 15% above Rs 1 crore, 25% above Rs 2 crore, 37% above Rs 5 crore.
15. Surcharge marginal relief: tax plus surcharge cannot exceed (tax payable on income equal to the threshold, plus the surcharge at the previous tier's rate on that tax) plus the income above the threshold.

## D. Deductions (old regime only)
16. HRA exemption (Sec 10(13A), Rule 2A): the least of (a) HRA actually received, (b) rent paid minus 10% of basic salary, (c) 50% of basic salary if the city is Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata or Chennai, otherwise 40%. All figures annual. Basic salary includes dearness allowance. If (b) is negative the exemption is nil.
17. Sec 80C: maximum Rs 1,50,000.
18. Sec 80CCD(1B): additional NPS deduction, maximum Rs 50,000.
19. Sec 80D: health insurance premium, up to Rs 25,000 for self, spouse and children, raised to Rs 50,000 if the person is 60 or above. Separately, up to Rs 25,000 for parents, raised to Rs 50,000 if the parents are 60 or above.
20. Sec 24(b): interest on a home loan for a self-occupied house, maximum Rs 2,00,000.
